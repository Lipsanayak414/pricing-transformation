# Deal Desk Approval Matrix & Discount Governance

> A **designed policy framework** bounded by published SaaS discounting benchmarks — not an
> analysis of transaction data. It defines who may approve what, and the guardrails no deal may cross.

## 1. Discount approval authority (by discount depth)

| Discount on list | Approval authority | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 10% | Sales Rep | Routine; within standard latitude. |
| 10 – 20% | Sales Manager | Margin impact begins; needs management sign-off. |
| 20 – 30% | VP Sales + Head of Pricing | Material margin risk; pricing must co-approve. |
| > 30% | CFO | Exceptional; P&L-level decision. |

## 2. Modifiers (escalate one tier when any apply)

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| DWP Tier 1 carrier (>$5B) | Escalate one tier — strategic, precedent-setting. |
| Multi-year term > 3 years | Escalate one tier — locks pricing for longer. |
| Net-new logo (vs renewal) | Escalate one tier — sets the reference price. |
| Migration deal with credits | Head of Pricing co-approval regardless of band. |

## 3. Migration-specific pricing rules

- Recognition of prior investment is given via **one-time migration credits or implementation
  concessions**, never as a permanent reduction to the subscription price.
- Strategic accounts may receive a **step-up ramp** (discount declining over the term), not a
  standing discount.
- Conversion price is set as a multiple of prior annual maintenance ARR (target 1.25x–2.0x by
  segment), per the scenario model.

## 4. Guardrails (no deal may breach without CFO approval)

| Guardrail | Threshold | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription gross-margin floor | ≥ 55% | Model margin band (cloud COGS reality). |
| Net dollar retention | maintain 108–120% band | Duck Creek disclosed range. |
| Maximum standing discount | 30% (above → CFO) | This matrix. |
| Credit as % of first-year ARR | ≤ 25% | Governance policy. |
